About

I’m Afonso Matos da Cruz, a graphic design student from Ghent. I like working with typography, image and colour, and I pay attention to detail and clear composition.

My focus right now is branding, web design and visual communication. Photography and film appeal to me too: moving image lets you tell a story or carry a feeling.

I find inspiration in architecture, technology, photography, film, fashion and other creatives’ work. Outside school I go to the gym, love to travel and watch films, series and documentaries, and listen to a lot of music.

I’m looking for a creative environment where I can learn, and where I can take feedback on board. My goal isn’t only to gain experience, but also to contribute to projects and grow as a designer.
