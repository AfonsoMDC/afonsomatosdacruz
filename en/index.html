About

I’m Afonso, a student in Ghent. I like working with typography, image and colour, and I pay attention to detail.

I started with STEM techniques, then studied Graphic Techniques, and now I study Crossmedia at Don Bosco Sint-Denijs-Westrem. In my school projects I make posters, editorial layouts, visual identities, small websites, photos and films.

I want to understand why a design works, and I keep refining my approach through school projects and personal work. Outside school I play sports, like to travel and follow technology.

I’m looking for a place where I can learn, take feedback on board and contribute to real projects.
