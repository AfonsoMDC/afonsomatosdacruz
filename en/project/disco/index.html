Disco Revisited

Rhythm, light and freedom

Type
School project
School
Don Bosco Sint-Denijs-Westrem, Ghent · 4D/A Graphic Techniques
When
2025
Made
Moodboards, editorial spread, motion design and two websites

A project about disco as visual culture: big typography, mirror balls, colour and rhythm. Starting from the film Saturday Night Fever (1977), I developed an editorial spread, a short motion design video and two websites, one of them about the Memphis group.

01

Moodboards

The project started with image: two moodboards collecting colours, shapes and atmosphere from which everything else was built.

02

Editorial spread

A double page with the title ‘DISCO’ in outline letters, a yellow-orange subtitle ‘Ritme, licht en vrijheid’, a violet field with a quote and a cut-out mirror-ball figure in the middle. The text runs in columns and boxes around the images: from underground to mainstream, the sound, image and style, and the 12-inch single.

03

Disco in motion

A short motion design video inspired by Saturday Night Fever. The video is also shown under Film.

04

From spread to HTML

The spread translated to the web: a dark page with yellow subheadings, the video and a box about the 12-inch single. Built with HTML and CSS, shown here as a screenshot.

05

Memphis design

A second site about the Memphis movement, which began in Italy in 1981 around Ettore Sottsass: bright colours, geometric shapes and patterns. Built with HTML and CSS, shown here as a screenshot.
