Seaside Bathing Culture in Ostend

From bathing machine to beach club

Type
School project
School
Don Bosco Sint-Denijs-Westrem, Ghent · 4D/A Graphic Techniques
Made
Style guide, editorial spread, illustration with animation and two websites

A project about the history of bathing culture in Ostend, from the Belle Époque to today. I built a visual identity around it: a style guide with typography and colour, an editorial spread, an illustration with animation and two websites.

01

Visual style guide

The foundation of the project, in four parts: typography, colour palette, colour variations and visual identity. Bodoni Moda is used for titles and accents, Lora for running text. The colours come from the coast: Storm Gray, Mist White and Sand Beige as the base, supplemented by Slate Blue, Ochre Gold and Cream Ivory, with Rust Red as a sparing accent.

02

Editorial spread

A double page in the style of the guide: a large title in Bodoni Moda, a bathing machine as a cut-out image, wavy lines and columns about the birth of the seaside resort, elegance on the beach and the evolution of swimwear. At the bottom, a contrast between then and now.

03

Character and animation

A vector illustration of a figure with a black hat and blue apron, with zigzag lines as texture, and a short animation of it. The animation is also shown under Film.

04

Ostend — City by the Sea

A tourist site about Ostend with eight places you should not miss, a section about the city and a contact form. Built with HTML and CSS, shown here as a screenshot.

05

Seaside Bathing Culture in Ostend

A storytelling site in the style of the style guide, with the sections Story, Evolution and Image archive. Built with HTML and CSS, shown here as a screenshot.
