Privacy and rights

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who is responsible?

Afonso Matos da Cruz, Ghent, Belgium. Contact: afonsomatos820@gmail.com.

What data does this website collect?

None. The website has no contact form, no cookies, no analytics or tracking services, and stores nothing in your browser. That is why there is no cookie banner.

The website’s hosting provider may keep technical log files, such as your IP address and the time of your visit. This is needed to run and secure the site. I have no access to your data myself, apart from what the hosting provider offers in its management environment.

Email

If you email me, I receive your email address and your message. I only use them to reply and do not keep them longer than necessary.

External links and services

  • Font: DM Sans is loaded from this website itself. There is no connection to Google Fonts.
  • Instagram and YouTube: these are plain links. Only when you click one do you go to their website, where their privacy rules apply.
  • School websites: the projects link to school websites I made. They are in the schoolwebsites folder and load fonts from Google Fonts, so your IP address may reach Google. This does not happen on the main website.

Your rights

You can ask what data I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Email me to do so. You can also file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).

Copyright

The designs, photos, films and texts on this website were made by me as school assignments or personal projects. Please do not copy or reuse them without my permission.

Some designs use other people’s material, such as photographs or artworks, as part of a school assignment. The rights to that material remain with the rights holders. If you would like something changed or removed, email me and I will deal with it quickly.
